HOW OPENGOV INDIA WORKS

One record. Multiple perspectives.

01

Government record

Project, budget, department, timeline and official evidence are attributed to their source.

02

Public visibility

Citizens can search projects, compare fund stages and understand implementation progress.

03

Ground report

Citizens can submit observations separately, so public reports are not confused with official updates.

04

Review & action

A future government integration can route reports to the responsible authority with an auditable status trail.

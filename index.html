HOW OPENGOV INDIA WORKS

One project record. Multiple perspectives.

01

Government record

Project, budget, timeline and official evidence are attributed to their source.

02

Public visibility

Citizens can search projects, understand funding stages and see implementation progress.

03

Ground report

Citizens can submit observations separately, so public reports are not confused with official updates.

04

Review & action

Future integrations can route reviewed cases to the responsible authority with a traceable status trail.

AI-ASSISTED INTELLIGENCE

Turn long records into useful questions.

Gemini can assist authorized reviewers by summarizing available documents, comparing project information and highlighting items that may need human attention. AI does not make official findings.

Human verification required

AI output is decision support, not an accusation, audit finding or government determination.

FOR GOVERNMENT

A monitoring layer, not a replacement.

OpenGov is designed to complement existing systems by organizing project information, surfacing items for attention and creating a structured path from public observation to human review.

Open authorized dashboard →

FOR ENTERPRISE

Private project intelligence.

A separate enterprise product can give real-estate, construction and infrastructure companies private project dashboards for budgets, milestones, documents, site evidence and AI-assisted reporting.
